1 — Overview

What is CalKit?

CalKit is a browser-based financial tool for teams. It calculates invoice splits across divisions and breaks down IT division costs by hours worked per job — no installation required.

🧾
Invoice Split
Split total sales by division percentage, with OS cost deduction before splitting.
⚙️
IT Job Costs
Distribute the IT division's amount proportionally across job items by hours.
💱
FX Converter
Convert foreign currency to THB — useful for entering overseas OS costs.
🧮
Calculator
Built-in calculator. Supports keyboard input when no text field is focused.
🕐
History
Every calculation is saved in-session. Search and reload any past project.
📋
Copy Anywhere
Copy any amount or job title with one click — paste directly into Excel or other systems.

2.3 — Managing OS Price

OS Price is a cost deducted from total sales before the percentage split is applied. After splitting, the OS amount is returned to the designated division.

1
Enable the "Has OS Price?" toggle
The system automatically creates the first OS entry when you turn this on.
2
Select OS type: External or Internal
External — Cost from an outside vendor or supplier. Add an optional name (e.g. "Vendor A").
Internal — Cost attributed to an internal division (e.g. DMD handled the work). Select the division from the dropdown.
3
Enter the OS Amount (THB)
Type the OS cost in Thai Baht. If the cost is in a foreign currency, use the FX Converter (right panel) to convert it first.
4
Select "Return to" division
Choose which division receives the OS amount back after the net split. Typically the division that managed or handled the OS cost.
Example — Internal OS Scenario:
POC project: Sales is the lead contact, TED assists, DMD's work is treated as an internal OS cost.
→ Select Internal OS → Division = DMD → Return to = Sales
→ System deducts DMD cost, splits remaining net 80:20 between Sales and TED, then adds DMD cost back to Sales.
📊 Example Calculation with Internal OS
Total Sales฿300,000.00
Internal OS (DMD) — deducted−฿50,000.00
Net Amount for splitting฿250,000.00
Sales 80% × ฿250,000฿200,000.00
TED 20% × ฿250,000฿50,000.00

Grand Total฿300,000.00 ✓
💡 Multiple OS entries: Click "+ Add OS cost" to add as many entries as needed. Each entry has its own type, amount, and Return to selection — all deducted before the split and returned independently.

2.4 — Division & Split (%)

Select the relevant divisions and set percentage splits. All active divisions must total exactly 100%.

1
Tick the divisions involved
Choose from DMD, Sales, IT Production, TED, TPM — or click "+ Add division" to add a custom one.
2
Set the percentage using the slider or number field
Drag the slider, click a tick mark (10%, 20%… 100%), or type any value such as 73 directly in the input field on the right.
3
Check the total indicator
The "Total: X%" label turns red if not 100 and green when ready to calculate.
💡 Tip: If the total % is not 100 when you click Calculate, a popup alert will warn you before any calculation runs.

2.6 — FX Converter & Calculator

Both tools are always available in the bottom-right panel on every page.

FX Converter

Converts a foreign currency amount to Thai Baht for use in the OS Amount field.

1
Enter the foreign amount
Type the amount in the foreign currency (e.g. 5,000 USD).
2
Enter the exchange rate
Type how many THB one unit equals (e.g. 35.50).
3
Copy the result and paste into OS Amount
The THB result appears instantly. Click Copy, then paste it into the OS Amount field.

Calculator

A basic calculator supporting +, −, ×, ÷, %, and +/−.

MethodDescription
Click on screenTap numbers and operators as normal
Keyboard inputType numbers and operators when no input field is focused
EscapeClear all (same as AC)
BackspaceDelete the last digit entered
Enter or =Calculate the result

2.7 — Calculation History

Every time you click Calculate & Verify, the result is saved automatically in the current session. You can search for and reload any past calculation.

1
Type a project name or number in the search bar
Results filter in real-time as you type.
2
Click a result to reload it
All data (amounts, divisions, OS entries) are restored to the form instantly, ready to recalculate or review.
⚠️ Important: History is stored in memory only. It is permanently lost when you close or refresh the browser tab. Copy all required amounts before closing the page.
3 — IT Job Costs

IT Job Costs Calculator

Use this page to distribute the IT division's invoice amount across job items, proportionally based on hours worked on each task.

1
Go to IT Job Costs
Click IT Job Costs in the navbar — or click the → Break down IT job costs button in the Invoice Summary. The IT amount is pre-filled automatically when using the button.
2
Enter IT Division Amount
Type the total amount IT received (THB). If you arrived via the lead button, this is already filled in.
3
Add job items
Click "+ Add job item" and enter the job title and hours for each task. The Total hrs counter updates live as you type.
4
Review the breakdown on the right
The system instantly shows Rate/hr, each job's % of total hours, and its calculated cost. ✓ Correct confirms the total matches.
5
Copy amounts or job titles
Use Copy ฿ to copy a job's cost, or Copy title to copy the job name — paste directly into any report or system.

4 — Tips & Best Practices

Tips & Best Practices

✅
Check % before calculating
The Total % indicator must be green (100.0%) before clicking Calculate.
📋
Copy amounts before closing
History is session-only. Copy all needed figures immediately after calculating.
💱
Convert FX before entering OS
Use the FX Converter to get the THB value, then Copy and paste it into the OS Amount field.
🔗
Use the IT lead button
The "→ Break down IT job costs" button in the summary carries the IT amount across automatically.
📱
Works on mobile
Fully responsive. The calculator can be collapsed with the Show/Hide button to save space.
🔒
Data stays local
All calculations run in your browser. No data is ever sent to any server.
📌 Recommended Workflow:
1. Fill in Project Info (optional) → 2. Enter total sales → 3. Add OS entries if applicable → 4. Select divisions & set % → 5. Click Calculate & Verify → 6. Copy each division's amount → 7. Click the IT lead button if IT is involved → 8. Add job items with hours → 9. Copy job costs
